Gombe Government Signs Contracts for 97km Road Projects Across Four LGAs

By Trending9ja Staff • November 23, 2026 • 1 min read

The Gombe State Government has finalized agreements for seven road construction projects spanning approximately 97 kilometres across four local government areas. According to Vanguard, the targeted regions include Balanga, Kaltungo, Shongom, and Yamaltu-Deba, with the aim of linking rural communities and boosting trade.

Speaking at the signing ceremony organized by the Ministry of Works and Infrastructure, the Commissioner, Hon. Shehu Yerima Abdullahi, stated that the works form part of Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya's development agenda. He noted that the initiative focuses on expanding infrastructure into underserved areas and improving the welfare of rural dwellers.

Vanguard reported that the commissioner instructed the contractors to comply strictly with approved engineering standards and contractual obligations. He emphasized that timely completion and high quality remain mandatory, assuring that officials will monitor the sites to ensure compliance.

Representatives from the selected construction firms, including EEC International Limited, SWAS GRADE Limited, and DATUM Construction Nigeria Limited, appreciated the state for the contract awards. They committed to executing quality work within the agreed schedules.

The list of approved works features the 38-kilometre Lapan-Labariya-Lasanjan-Latatar-Lakunturum Road with a spur to Burak, the 18.9-kilometre Talasse-Bangu-Boka Bundi-Walalunguda Road, the 15-kilometre Bangunji-Dadiya-Yelwa-Gombe/Yola Road, and the 10.5-kilometre Dogon Ruwa-Kaltin Road. Additional projects include the nine-kilometre Kurjale-Pata Road, Phase One of the four-kilometre Degri-Sikam-Nyuwar Kindiyo Road, and the 1.5-kilometre Kunuwal Road Network.

The state executive council approved the contracts during a recent meeting chaired by Governor Yahaya. Once completed, the upgraded networks are anticipated to ease travel, assist farmers in transporting produce to markets, and enhance economic activities across the benefiting communities.

Sources: Vanguard
