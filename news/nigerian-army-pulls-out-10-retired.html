Nigerian Army Pulls Out 10 Retired Generals in Bauchi Parade

By Trending9ja Staff • November 22, 2026 • 1 min read

The Nigerian Army Armoured Corps has formally conducted a pulling-out ceremony for 10 retired senior officers in Bauchi State, Punch reported. The event took place on Saturday at the Brigadier General Y. Abubakar Parade Ground located within Obienu Barracks.

The ceremony marked the conclusion of the week-long 2026 Nigerian Army Armoured Corps Biennial Conference and Reunion. Major Adekunle Akinleye, acting spokesperson for the Armoured Corps headquarters, announced the details in an official statement released on Sunday.

The senior officers honoured during the event included Lieutenant General Abdulsalami Ibrahim, alongside Major Generals Benjamin Sawyerr, Ibrahim Ali, Sani Mohammed, and Ibrahim Jallo. Brigadier Generals Victor Ita, Tuni Isa, Aminu Usman, Ezra Barkins, and Suleiman Adamu were also among the retirees.

Serving as the special guest of honour and reviewing officer, retired Lieutenant General Ibrahim stated that the Armoured Corps represents a dedicated way of life beyond being a military branch. He encouraged serving personnel, especially younger officers, to remain disciplined, courageous, and professional while offering appreciation to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu and the military command for their support.

Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu, the Chief of Army Staff, was represented at the occasion by Major General Godwin Mutkut. The event featured a ceremonial march, souvenir presentations, and group photography with serving personnel, family members, and well-wishers in attendance.

Sources: Punch
