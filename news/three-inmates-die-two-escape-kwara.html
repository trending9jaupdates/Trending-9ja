Three Inmates Die, Two Escape After Kwara Custodial Centre Jailbreak Attempt

By Trending9ja Staff • November 25, 2026 • 1 min read

Three condemned prisoners have died and two others escaped following a jailbreak attempt at the Oke Kura facility in Ilorin, Kwara State. According to reports by Punch and Channels TV, seven death-row inmates attempted to breach security at the location, but officers intercepted five of them while two successfully fled.

The Controller-General of the Nigerian Correctional Service, Sylvester Nwakuche, confirmed the deaths during a visit to Ilorin on Wednesday. He stated that he was mandated by the Minister of Interior, Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo, to assess the situation. Nwakuche noted that three of the intercepted prisoners lost their lives under questionable circumstances, while the other two are receiving treatment at an unnamed government hospital.

Sources within the facility told the media that about 20 officers were questioned over the security breach, with some queried specifically about the deaths of the inmates. Nwakuche explained that a panel involving medical, clinical, and intelligence experts has been set up to investigate the deaths, promising that any staff member found guilty of wrongdoing will face legal action.

The correctional chief added that security forces are collaborating to recapture the two escaped prisoners. He further expressed concern over severe overcrowding at the Oke Kura facility, which currently holds around 685 detainees, including more than 70 inmates sentenced to death, some of whom have spent over ten years awaiting execution.

Nwakuche disclosed that Nigeria has over 4,000 prisoners on death row nationwide. He urged state governors to commute death sentences to life imprisonment where execution warrants cannot be carried out, noting that this would enable the service to transfer inmates to less congested facilities for rehabilitation.

Sources: Punch · Channels TV
