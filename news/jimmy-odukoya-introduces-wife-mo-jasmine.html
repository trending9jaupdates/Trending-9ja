Jimmy Odukoya Introduces Wife Mo Jasmine as Fountain of Life Church First Lady

By Trending9ja Staff • November 2, 2026 • 1 min read

The Senior Pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, Jimmy Odukoya, has officially presented his wife, Mo Jasmine Daniel, to the congregation as the ministry's new first lady. The announcement took place during a service at the church headquarters located in Ilupeju, Lagos State, according to a report by BellaNaija.

Before calling her up to the stage, Pastor Jimmy revealed to the worshippers that he had been navigating his personal life alone after assuming the leadership of the ministry. He explained that he had searched for a partner to share his life's journey with before inviting her to join him at the altar amidst cheers from members.

Addressing the congregation during the service, Pastor Mo Odukoya expressed her delight and noted that it was an honour for her to become part of the church family. She is the daughter of Reverend Theophilus and Bolaji Daniel.

The publication observed that the couple appeared in matching outfits for the presentation. Pastor Jimmy wore a structured burnt-orange native outfit featuring dark cuff accents and a necklace, while Pastor Mo put on a patterned orange and copper floor-length dress paired with a matching clutch bag.

Pastor Jimmy assumed leadership of the church in 2023 after the passing of his father, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, who co-founded the church with his late wife, Pastor Bimbo Odukoya. Apart from his ministry work, Jimmy is an actor and musical artist who appeared as Oba Ade in the 2022 movie The Woman King.

Sources: BellaNaija
