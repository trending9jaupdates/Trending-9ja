APC Youth Group Demands Clarification from Senator Yari Over Hunger Remarks

By Trending9ja Staff • November 22, 2026 • 2 min read

A political group known as the North Central APC Youths Progressive Forum has called on former Zamfara State governor, Senator Abdul'aziz Abubakar Yari, to publicly address comments attributed to him regarding hunger and hard work. According to a report by Legit.ng, the forum insisted that the lawmaker needs to set the record straight amid prevailing economic challenges in the country.

In a press release issued by its convener, Sadiq Yahaya Kwande, the youth wing stressed that starvation must never be equated with idleness, adding that poverty should not be seen as an individual shortcoming. The organisation argued that government figures ought to demonstrate greater consideration when talking about the financial pressures confronting ordinary citizens across Nigeria.

The forum highlighted several hardworking segments of society who are currently battling harsh realities through no fault of their own. These include farmers stopped from accessing their fields due to insecurity, jobless university graduates, medical practitioners working under intense strain, and teachers struggling to survive on earnings that cannot meet basic family requirements.

Pointing out that citizens under the age of 30 make up roughly 70 percent of the national population, the group stated that young people require support and empowerment rather than dismissive comments. While noting that the senator and his aides had presented differing interpretations of his words, the forum insisted on a formal clarification aired on major television networks, including NTA, Channels-competitor TVC, AIT, and Arise News, as well as print newspapers.

The youth coalition also used the opportunity to advise the national leadership of the All Progressives Congress to resolve internal party disputes through standard and fair channels. It cautioned against judging party members based on family ties without substantiated proof, urging fair hearings whenever grievances are filed.

Finally, the group advised veteran party leaders to integrate young media practitioners and policy aides into their teams to ensure public speeches reflect real-world economic realities. It similarly urged President Bola Ahmed Tinubu to promote responsible communication among top officials connected to his administration.

Sources: Legit.ng
