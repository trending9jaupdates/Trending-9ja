IGP Disu Promises Professional Police Conduct Ahead of 2027 Polls

By Trending9ja Staff • October 15, 2026 • 2 min read

File image

Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Disu has assured Nigerians that the force will perform professionally before, during, and after the 2027 general elections.

“We’ve learnt from past elections. I give you my word,” Disu said, promising conduct that would make Nigerians proud. The assurance comes as political activities intensify and INEC prepares for the polls.

Sources: Naija News
