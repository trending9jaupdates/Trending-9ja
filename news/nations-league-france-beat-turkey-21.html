Nations League: France Beat Turkey 2-1 To Finish Top Of Group A1

By Trending9ja Staff • November 15, 2026 • 1 min read

France secured the top spot in UEFA Nations League Group A1 after recording a 2-1 victory over Turkey at the Stade Atlantique in Bordeaux on November 15, 2026, according to Vanguard.

The victory enabled the French team to bounce back from their recent defeat to Italy and finish at the summit of the group standings. On the other hand, Turkey finished at the very bottom of the table after the final round of fixtures.

France broke the deadlock shortly after the half-time interval through an own goal scored by Turkish player Emirhan Topcu. Eli Junior Kroupi then doubled the advantage for the home side, marking his very first goal for the French national team.

Thanks to the outcome, the team managed by Zinedine Zidane will enter the last-eight draw on Friday in Nyon, Switzerland, as a seeded team. While this status ensures France will host the return leg of their quarter-final tie in March 2027, it also leaves open the possibility of a fixture against Spain.

Sources: Vanguard
