Tonto Dikeh Dedicates New Church Building to Honor Pastor Jerry Eze

By Trending9ja Staff • November 14, 2026 • 1 min read

Nollywood star turned evangelist Tonto Dikeh has officially commissioned a church building in honor of Pastor Jerry Eze, the founder of Streams of Joy International. According to Punch, the actress announced the development on Saturday after sharing pictures from the event on her Instagram account.

During the ceremony held at the New Wine Prophetic Tabernacle, Dikeh appeared in a red robe bearing the inscription "ELROI". A message on the dedication plaque noted that the structure was commissioned by Evangelist Tonto Dikeh under the leadership of Prophet Bala Jagaba on November 14, 2026, to honor Pastor Eze.

The event follows Dikeh's launch of her interdenominational prayer and revival fellowship, known as the New Wine Prophetic Tabernacle Global ministry, in August 2026. Punch had previously reported that the actress created the platform as part of her expanding Christian activities.

Prior to the commissioning, Dikeh shared photos and video clips in October 2026 showing the foundation-laying ceremony for the project. During that initial phase, building materials were anointed amidst prayers, and she dedicated the construction to God while publicly acknowledging Eze as her spiritual father.

Pastor Jerry Eze is widely known for leading Streams of Joy International and convening the New Season Prophetic Prayers and Declarations, a popular digital prayer platform with global followers. Dikeh has increasingly prioritized her faith and evangelism alongside her career in the movie industry.

Sources: Punch
