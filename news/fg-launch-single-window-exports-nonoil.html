FG to Launch Single Window for Exports as Non-Oil Trade Hits $6.1bn

By Trending9ja Staff • November 17, 2026 • 1 min read

The Federal Government has announced plans to streamline export paperwork and regulatory clearances starting December 14, 2026. The move aims to sustain the momentum in Nigeria's non-oil export sector, which generated a record $6.1 billion in 2025.

Tola Fakolade, the Director of the National Single Window Project, shared the details during a stakeholders' meeting in Abuja, according to Vanguard. He explained that the upcoming second phase of the initiative will offer local traders a unified digital system to handle regulatory licences, certificates, and operational permits.

Fakolade noted that rather than moving between various government offices for clearances, merchants will submit documents through a single portal. He revealed that the first phase, which focused on import licences, has successfully cleared more than 169,000 permits and certificates since its rollout.

Providing context on the trade figures, Nonye Ayeni, the Executive Director of the Nigerian Export Promotion Council, reported that non-oil earnings climbed by 11.5 percent to $6.1 billion in 2025, with shipment volumes growing by 10 percent to 8.02 million metric tonnes. Nigeria sent 281 different goods to 120 destinations worldwide, including 36 nations across Africa.

Ayeni pointed out that long regulatory hurdles and duplicated paperwork often lead to cancelled deals, extra costs, and spoiled perishable goods. To resolve this, she confirmed that the council is linking its electronic registration and Export Expansion Grant systems directly into the single window to end the manual movement of files.

Also addressing the forum, the Chairman of the Nigeria Revenue Service, Zach Adedeji, represented by Alfred Okoh, described the project as a comprehensive overhaul of national trade operations rather than just a technology upgrade, pledging continuous technical support for the rollout.

Sources: Vanguard
