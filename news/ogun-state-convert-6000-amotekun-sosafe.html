Ogun State to convert 6,000 Amotekun, So-Safe officers to state police

By Trending9ja Staff • November 8, 2026 • 1 min read

Ogun State Governor Dapo Abiodun has declared that the state is fully prepared to launch its own local police service, leveraging over 6,000 existing officers from community security units. According to Vanguard, the governor explained that personnel currently serving in the Ogun State Security Network, commonly called Amotekun, as well as the So-Safe Corps, will provide the needed manpower.

The governor made this statement while attending the golden jubilee celebration of Ereke Day in Ikenne Local Government Area. He revealed that lawmakers in the Ogun State House of Assembly have given approval for the creation of state police, calling the decision a crucial milestone toward safeguarding lives and property.

Abiodun pointed out that his administration previously established solid community security structures, which will make the transition into a state police command smooth. Vanguard reported that the governor highlighted his leadership role as chairman of the Nigeria Governors’ Forum Committee on State Police Creation, where he also represents President Bola Tinubu.

Addressing residents at the event, Abiodun stressed that securing the state is essential for economic growth, sustainable development, and investor confidence. He urged local communities to maintain close cooperation with security organizations to ensure safety across business centers and residential areas.

In addition to security matters, the governor directed the immediate rebuilding of the Delabo-Ikenne Road to enhance connectivity and stimulate local economic activities. He praised traditional rulers and the Ikenne Community Development Association for sustaining the annual Ereke Day celebration for 50 years.

Sources: Vanguard
