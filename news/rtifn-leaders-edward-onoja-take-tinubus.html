RTIFN Leaders, Edward Onoja Take Tinubu's Campaign to Lokoja Streets

By Trending9ja Staff • November 11, 2026 • 1 min read

Prominent political leaders have taken to the streets of Lokoja, the Kogi State capital, to raise awareness for President Bola Tinubu's Renewed Hope initiative. The Director-General of the Relax, Tinubu Is Fixing Nigeria (RTIFN) group, Ahmed Bala, spearheaded the street campaign alongside former Kogi State Deputy Governor Edward Onoja and former APC National Women Leader Salamatu Baiwa Umar-Eluma.

According to Daily Post, the public mobilisation was structured to deepen grassroots structures for the organisation across Kogi State as preparations gather momentum for the 2027 general elections. The political gathering brought together local stakeholders, youth representatives, women groups, and community leaders to discuss federal development programmes and civic involvement.

As part of the lineup, the group officially inaugurated its Kogi State Executive Committee. This new leadership body is expected to manage the organisation’s operations and sustain public interaction across all 21 local government councils within the state.

The event also featured an empowerment scheme designed to assist local residents, especially youths and women. The group's executive team emphasized that the Renewed Hope Agenda must translate into tangible support for citizens through skill development, entrepreneurship, innovation, and agricultural opportunities.

The day's events were billed to conclude with a celebratory dinner at the Government House in Lokoja. RTIFN, which is affiliated with the City Boy Movement established by Seyi Tinubu, continues to conduct similar awareness drives across the country to mobilize support for the federal administration.

Sources: Daily Post
