Danielle Adewusi Unveils Self-Designed Benin-Inspired Outfit at Miss Universe

By Trending9ja Staff • November 24, 2026 • 1 min read

Miss Universe Nigeria 2026, Danielle Adewusi, has presented a self-designed national costume paying homage to the ancient Kingdom of Benin at the 75th Miss Universe pageant in Puerto Rico, according to BellaNaija.

The outfit, titled "Daughter of the Coral Throne," blends traditional motifs with contemporary African fashion. It features heavy red coral beadwork across a sheer-panelled corset bodysuit, gold-toned cowrie shell trim, and a sweeping floor-length train. The look is complemented by a fan-shaped headpiece, a circular halo backpiece with bead fringes, and a golden staff adorned with a Queen Idia mask emblem.

Adewusi stated that the ensemble was crafted to celebrate her Edo heritage and highlight the artistic traditions of Benin royalty. She explained that the golden ceremonial staff represents royal authority and pays tribute to the Oba of Benin, while the circular backpiece symbolizes ancestral continuity across generations.

The 29-year-old medical practitioner and King’s College London graduate won the Miss Universe Nigeria title in September. Her participation follows in the footsteps of her mother, Tonia Okogbenin, who represented Nigeria at the global Miss Universe contest 35 years prior in 1991.

In addition to her pageantry career, Adewusi runs a women's healthcare campaign called Scrub the Stigma. She is representing Nigeria alongside 116 contestants from around the world, including 17 delegates from Africa, as the competition approaches its final ceremony.

Sources: BellaNaija
