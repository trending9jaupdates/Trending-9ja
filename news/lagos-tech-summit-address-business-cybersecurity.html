Lagos Tech Summit To Address Business Cybersecurity Risks In Africa

By Trending9ja Staff • November 23, 2026 • 1 min read

Lagos is set to host an upcoming conference focused on addressing cyber threats and digital risk management facing African corporate entities, Punch reported. The gathering, organized by IT consulting outfit Digital Jewels Africa, marks the firm's 18th anniversary and takes place on November 25.

The event bears the theme 'The Trust Imperative: Securing Africa's Digital Future.' It aims to bring together policymakers, corporate executives, and technology specialists from sectors including banking, telecommunications, energy, and insurance. Discussions will center on protecting corporate data and building user trust as organizations expand their reliance on artificial intelligence and digital channels.

According to organizers, the conference will be chaired by Ibukun Awosika, Chair of Digital Jewels Africa, with Tayo Aduloju, Managing Director of the Nigerian Economic Summit Group, delivering the keynote address. The host of the meeting will be Adedoyin Odunfa, who serves as the founder and Chief Executive Officer of the consulting firm.

Several prominent business leaders are scheduled to participate in the event. These include Union Bank Managing Director Yetunde Oni, Leadway Assurance Managing Director Gboyega Lesi, eTranzact International CEO Niyi Toluwalope, and Midwestern Oil & Gas Managing Director Elozino Olaniyan. Officials such as Usman Usman, Director of Shared Services at the Central Bank of Nigeria, are also slated to attend.

Cybersecurity leaders participating in panel sessions include Dr. Bharat Soni of GTCO, Dr. Favour Femi-Oyewole of Access Bank, Adebayo Adebeshin of Interswitch Group, and Jude Anietie of MTN Nigeria. Punch noted that participants will examine the collective duties of regulators, executives, and technical experts in safeguarding sensitive corporate infrastructure while navigating growing technology risks.

Sources: Punch
