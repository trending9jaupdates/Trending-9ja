UNN Confirms Death of Four Students After Off-Campus Party in Enugu

By Trending9ja Staff • November 12, 2026 • 1 min read

Four students from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) have passed away following an off-campus gathering in Nsukka, Enugu State, according to reports by Channels TV and Premium Times.

The Vice-Chancellor of the institution, Professor Simon Ortuanya, stated during a briefing with journalists that ten students were involved in the event, which took place on November 11, 2026. The gathering was organized to celebrate a student who had just finished writing final-year examinations.

Channels TV reported that four of the attendees died after allegedly ingesting substances suspected to be drugs and alcohol. The six other students who survived the incident are currently receiving treatment at different medical facilities.

Professor Ortuanya also clarified that the affected individuals belonged to multiple academic departments, refuting earlier claims suggesting that all the victims were studying in the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences.

Describing the loss as tragic, the Vice-Chancellor declared a three-day period of mourning across the university community to honour the deceased students.

Sources: Premium Times · Channels TV
