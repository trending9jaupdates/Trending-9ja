Ireland to End Short-Stay Visa Waiver Scheme for 5 Countries by December 2026

By Trending9ja Staff • November 25, 2026 • 1 min read

According to Legit.ng, the Irish government has announced that its Short-Stay Visa Waiver Programme will officially close on December 31, 2026. Authorities have not indicated any plans to prolong the travel scheme beyond that date for the five participating countries.

The waiver policy currently applies to eligible citizens from Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain. Under the arrangement, travellers from these five Gulf nations can enter Ireland for short visits without securing a dedicated Irish visa, provided they already hold a valid United Kingdom visa or residence permit.

Legit.ng reported that the programme was recently given a brief extension from an earlier deadline of October 31, 2026, pushing the closing date to December 31, 2026. However, this is expected to be the final extension, as no replacement policy or further continuation has been confirmed by Irish authorities.

Consequently, visitors from the affected countries who intend to travel to Ireland in 2027 or beyond will likely need to apply for a standard Irish visa. Affected travellers have been advised to monitor official updates and arrange their documentation well in advance to prevent any disruption to their travel plans.

The Irish government has not provided any official explanation for its decision to end the programme after December 2026, and no substitute travel arrangements have been introduced at this time.

Sources: Legit.ng
