No Nigerian Student Will Miss University Due To Poverty, Tinubu Assures

By Trending9ja Staff • November 21, 2026 • 1 min read

President Bola Tinubu has assured Nigerians that financial difficulties will no longer stop any student from obtaining higher education in the country. He gave this assurance during the 37th convocation of the Federal University of Technology, Akure, according to Vanguard.

Represented at the event by Professor Olatunde Peter Ajagbonna of the National Universities Commission, the President stated that the Nigerian Education Loan Fund was set up to remove financial obstacles for tertiary students. He noted that the scheme provides financial backing and upkeep stipends for eligible candidates in public institutions.

Tinubu added that his government is determined to prevent strikes and ensure smooth academic calendars. He disclosed that agreements signed with various university staff unions have received full financial backing for implementation to guarantee worker welfare and industrial peace.

The President challenged university workers to show commitment to their duties, while urging institutions such as FUTA to lead in developing home-grown technologies. He stressed that stronger research and productive industry collaborations are vital for building a knowledge-based economy.

Directing his message to the graduating class, Tinubu urged them to be enterprising and use their skills to create opportunities despite present national challenges. FUTA Vice-Chancellor Professor Adenike Oladiji commended the Federal Government for intervention projects that she said have enhanced the institution's infrastructure and research capacity.

Sources: Vanguard
