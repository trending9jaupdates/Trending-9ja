2027: Utomi, Yesufu, Ohaa Rally Support For Obi-Kwankwaso Ticket

By Trending9ja Staff • October 29, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Vanguard

Prominent leaders of the Nigeria Democratic Congress have called on citizens to rally grassroots backup for Peter Obi's presidential bid in 2027. Speaking at a town hall event in Enugu State, stakeholders urged voters to unite behind Obi and his running partner, Rabiu Kwankwaso, according to Vanguard.

Political economist Pat Utomi stated that the joint ticket possesses the needed vision to tackle the nation's economic and security struggles. Similarly, Enugu governorship candidate Chinyeaka Ohaa noted that the political partnership offers an avenue to bridge national divides and foster inclusive governance across every region.

Activist Aisha Yesufu tasked citizens with taking personal charge of the upcoming election cycle. She charged supporters to bring out a minimum of twenty voters each for the candidate, stressing that ordinary Nigerians must actively work to secure electoral victory and safeguard their cast ballots.
