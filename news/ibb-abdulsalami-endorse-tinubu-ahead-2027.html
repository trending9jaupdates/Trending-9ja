IBB and Abdulsalami Endorse Tinubu Ahead of 2027 Polls, Says Gbajabiamila

By Trending9ja Staff • November 27, 2026 • 1 min read

Femi Gbajabiamila, the Chief of Staff to President Bola Tinubu, has disclosed that former military ruler General Ibrahim Babangida has backed the president ahead of the 2027 general elections. According to a report by Vanguard, Gbajabiamila also stated that former Head of State General Abdulsalami Abubakar evaluated the current administration positively.

The presidential chief of staff made the announcement while speaking to journalists in Minna, Niger State, following a visit by a federal delegation to the residences of the two former leaders. Gbajabiamila stated that Babangida expressed satisfaction with the president's current efforts and offered his full support for the upcoming elections.

Gbajabiamila further noted that Abdulsalami expressed approval of Tinubu's performance, remarking that the president has done well so far. Speaking on behalf of the National Peace Committee, the retired general called on Nigerians to embrace harmony, adding that peaceful conditions are essential for conducting successful elections in the country.

The visit was dispatched on the instructions of President Tinubu following his return from Borno State to review the wellbeing of the former leaders. The high-level delegation sent to Minna included Information Minister Mohammed Idris, Special Adviser Hadiza Bala-Usman, alongside former ministers Sa'idu Alkali and Yusuf Sununu.

Expressing gratitude for the gesture, Abdulsalami thanked President Tinubu for keeping them in mind despite his demanding governance duties. He also appreciated the presidency's support during his previous medical trip overseas, while confirming that both he and Babangida are currently enjoying improved health.

Sources: Vanguard
