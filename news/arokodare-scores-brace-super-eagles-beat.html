Arokodare Scores Brace as Super Eagles Beat Tanzania 3-1 in AFCON Qualifier

By Trending9ja Staff • November 14, 2026 • 1 min read

Nigeria kept their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification ambitions on track following a 3-1 victory over Tanzania at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo on Friday, according to Punch.

The Super Eagles grabbed an early lead in the second minute when Alhassan Yusuf finished off a team move initiated by Akor Adams and Ademola Lookman. However, the visiting side fought back to equalize in the 32nd minute after Novatus Miroshi took the ball from team captain Wilfred Ndidi and set up Alphonce Msanga to score. Nigeria was dealt a setback prior to the interval when Lookman left the pitch due to an apparent hamstring problem.

Head coach Eric Chelle introduced substitute Tolu Arokodare alongside Moses Usor at the start of the second half. The tactical move proved decisive as Arokodare put the hosts back ahead in the 52nd minute by flicking in a cutback from Ndidi. The Ajax forward then extended the lead shortly after, driving his second goal of the match past Tanzania goalkeeper Aishi Manula.

Following the win, Nigeria sits second in Group L with six points, trailing group leaders Guinea-Bissau by one point. Tanzania remains at the bottom of the table with one point, though they have already secured automatic entry to the tournament as co-hosts. The remaining qualification spot in the group is being contested by Nigeria, Guinea-Bissau, and Madagascar.

The Super Eagles will travel to face Tanzania again at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar on Tuesday, November 17, with kickoff scheduled for 2:00 pm Nigerian time.

Sources: Punch
