Tinubu Flags Off $3.5bn Bakassi Deep Seaport Project in Cross River

By Trending9ja Staff • November 13, 2026 • 1 min read

President Bola Tinubu has officially launched the construction of the Bakassi Deep Seaport in Cross River State. According to Punch, the President described the maritime development as a strategic national project intended to expand commercial trade, attract new manufacturing industries, and create employment opportunities.

Vice President Kashim Shettima represented the President at the groundbreaking ceremony in Bakassi on Friday. Speaking on behalf of Tinubu, Shettima noted that the infrastructure would establish new trade routes while enhancing Nigeria's capacity to handle maritime cargo within the West African sub-region.

Designed with a 20-metre draft capable of hosting large ocean vessels, the facility will be linked to a 260-kilometre superhighway to facilitate the transport of goods inland. The President stressed that the port would complement Lagos, which presently controls over 80 percent of the nation's international cargo traffic, rather than competing with it.

Originally conceived in 2014, the project progressed significantly following efforts by Governor Bassey Otu, Punch reported. These efforts include advancing a $3.5 billion financial agreement with the African Export-Import Bank and finalising its public-private partnership framework in February 2025.

Dignitaries present at the event included the Minister of Marine and Blue Economy, Gboyega Oyetola, Afreximbank Vice President Kanayo Aweni, Cross River Deputy Governor Peter Odey, and Senator Asuquo Ekpenyong Jr.

Reframing the history of the region following past territorial and oil revenue losses, Tinubu encouraged local residents to protect the investment from political interference, calling the seaport a vital driver for economic recovery.

Sources: Punch
