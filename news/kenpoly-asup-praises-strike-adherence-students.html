Kenpoly ASUP Praises Strike Adherence as Students Urge Fubara to Step In

By Trending9ja Staff • November 19, 2026 • 1 min read

Lecturers at the Kenule Beeson Saro-Wiwa Polytechnic in Bori have received commendation for strictly following an ongoing industrial action called by their union. Vanguard reported that the local branch leader praised staff solidarity as students separately appealed to the Rivers State administration for swift intervention.

Dr. Bariakawaadoo Bere, chairman of the Academic Staff Union of Polytechnics chapter at the institution, monitored the shutdown on Thursday. He noted that members demonstrated strong unity in demanding improved working conditions after state authorities failed to address their welfare concerns despite dialogue attempts and an expired ultimatum.

The industrial dispute follows a formal alert sent to Governor Siminalayi Fubara by the national body of ASUP before the strike started on November 16. The lecturers are pressing for the execution of an agreement reached on April 8, focusing mainly on the Consolidated Academic Tools Allowance and Earned Academic Allowance.

Bere highlighted that while university educators in the state had seen agreements fulfilled, polytechnic teachers faced unequal treatment over similar benefits. He maintained that lecturers are eager to resume teaching as soon as the government takes concrete steps to settle the disputed allowances, while urging affected students and parents to be patient.

In response to the closure, the Students’ Union Government pleaded with Governor Fubara to prevent a prolonged halt to learning. SUG President George Joseph Tams explained that over 80 percent of students had already traveled back to campus and incurred accommodation costs after classes began on November 2, only to be interrupted two weeks later.

Sources: Vanguard
