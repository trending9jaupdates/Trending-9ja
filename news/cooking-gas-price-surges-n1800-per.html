Cooking Gas Price Surges to N1,800 Per Kilogram in Nigeria

By Trending9ja Staff • November 13, 2026 • 1 min read

Household cooking gas costs have surged across Nigeria following recent price adjustments by depot operators and retail vendors over the past two days, forcing consumers to spend significantly more to refill their cylinders, according to Daily Post.

At the wholesale level, StockGap increased its depot price by N25, bringing the cost to N1,440 for each kilogram. Other major suppliers, including the Nigerian Independent Petroleum Company Limited (NIPCO), Mobil (now operating as 11 Plc), and Ardova, offered the commodity at N1,400 and N1,390 per kilogram respectively.

In Abuja and surrounding areas, retail stations such as Raniol, Juda Oil, and outlets owned by the Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited now sell Liquefied Petroleum Gas at rates between N1,550 and N1,600 per kilogram. This reflects a sharp increase from previous figures ranging from N1,400 to N1,500 per kilogram. Smaller neighbourhood micro-dealers in the federal capital are charging up to N1,700 or N1,800 per kilogram depending on their location.

The price hike directly impacts household budgets across the country. Daily Post reports that filling a standard 12.5kg cooking gas cylinder at N1,600 per kilogram now costs N20,000, compared to the N17,500 previously spent when the product sold at N1,400 per kilogram. This represents an immediate jump of N2,500 per refill.

Consumers may face additional financial pressure in the coming months. NIPCO spokesperson Taofeek Lawal previously disclosed to Daily Post that cooking gas prices could experience further upward adjustments between November and January next year.

Sources: Daily Post
