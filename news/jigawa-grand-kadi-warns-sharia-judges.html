Jigawa Grand Kadi Warns Shari’a Judges Against Detaining Litigants in Civil Cases

By Trending9ja Staff • November 20, 2026 • 1 min read

The Acting Grand Kadi of the Jigawa State Shari’a Court of Appeal, Kadi Umar Nasir Ahmad, has warned Shari’a Court judges in the state to stop remanding individuals involved in civil disputes unnecessarily. He emphasized that detaining accused persons over civil issues, especially monetary claims, violates fundamental principles of justice.

This directive was disclosed in an official release by Abbas Wangara, the Director of Information and Protocol Matters for the Jigawa State Judicial Service Commission, as reported by Punch. The statement was made available in Dutse following a familiarisation assessment of several judicial divisions across the state.

According to Punch, the inspection tour covered various locations, including the Shari’a Courts in Yalo, Kaugama, Madana, Kirikasamma, and Guri, alongside the Upper and Higher Shari’a Courts situated in Malam-Madori and Birniwa. The exercise aimed to evaluate operational performance and promote standard judicial practices among court personnel.

Addressing court clerks and registrars during the visits, Kadi Ahmad instructed them to keep accurate records of all court-imposed fines. He reminded the judicial workers that the court system exists to administer justice rather than operate as a revenue-generating entity, calling for total transparency and dedication in their duties.

Punch also reported that the Chief Registrar of the state Shari’a Court of Appeal, Barrister Kabiru Abubakar Gumel, advised court staff to maintain updated documentation. He further cautioned officers against taking leave or missing work without proper authorization.

The delegation accompanying Kadi Ahmad on the state tour included key judicial figures, such as the Chief Inspector of Shari’a Courts, Barrister Isma’il Sani, and the Deputy Chief Inspector for the Hadejia Zone, Barrister Isyak Mohd.

Sources: Punch
