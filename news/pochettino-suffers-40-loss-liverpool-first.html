Pochettino Suffers 4-0 Loss to Liverpool in First Manchester United Match

By Trending9ja Staff • November 22, 2026 • 1 min read

Mauricio Pochettino experienced a painful start as Manchester United manager as his side suffered a 4-0 defeat against Liverpool at Anfield, Channels TV reported. The outcome leaves the Red Devils second from the bottom of the Premier League table.

The game shifted dramatically in the eighth minute when United defender Leny Yoro received a red card. Referee Sam Barrott sent off the Frenchman after he clipped Alexander Isak, who had broken free following a mistake by Matthijs de Ligt on his first league start in nearly a year.

Liverpool took swift advantage of the extra player when Dominik Szoboszlai scored directly from the resulting free-kick. Minutes later, Florian Wirtz netted a second goal for the hosts by converting a cross from teenage winger Rio Ngumoha.

In an attempt to stabilize his defense, Pochettino brought on veteran defender Harry Maguire after 22 minutes and adjusted to a five-man backline. Despite the tactical change, Liverpool extended their lead in the second half through a finish from Ngumoha, before Victor Munoz completed the scoring from a Wirtz pass eight minutes from time.

Channels TV noted that the win pushed Liverpool into the Premier League top four, seven points behind leaders Arsenal. United, who hired Pochettino after sacking Michael Carrick following just one win in ten league games, have now conceded 34 goals across all competitions this season, marking the poorest defensive record across Europe's top five leagues.

Sources: Channels TV
