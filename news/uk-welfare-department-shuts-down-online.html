UK Welfare Department Shuts Down Online Portals Following Cyber Incident

By Trending9ja Staff • November 27, 2026 • 1 min read

The UK Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has suspended several of its online portals following a cyber incident detected within its digital infrastructure, according to Punch. The disruption has temporarily blocked public access to key digital welfare services across the United Kingdom.

The affected platforms include online portals used for managing state pensions, Universal Credit, and Personal Independence Payment applications. Punch reported that the UK government department took the systems offline on Friday as a precautionary measure after identifying the breach.

The DWP stated that it is currently collaborating with the UK National Cyber Security Centre and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre to investigate the incident and safely restore digital operations. According to Punch, the agency reassured pensioners and welfare claimants that their regular financial disbursements remain completely unaffected and will be processed on schedule.

Officials have not yet confirmed the precise nature of the cyber incident or indicated whether hackers compromised any personal information. As a safeguard, the department advised affected users whose online services are unavailable to attempt accessing the portals at a later time.

Punch also reported that the agency warned members of the public to stay vigilant against potential scam messages during the period of downtime. The DWP stressed that it does not send communications requesting recipients to supply personal bank details or click on external links.

Sources: Punch
