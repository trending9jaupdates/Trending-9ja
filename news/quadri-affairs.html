Yinka Quadri Says His Wife Knew About His Affairs With Actresses and Stayed Quiet

By Trending9ja Staff • October 10, 2026 • 3 min read

Veteran Yoruba movie actor Yinka Quadri has made a startling claim in an interview with African A List: that his wife knew about his relationships with actresses and chose not to confront them.

Quadri said female colleagues sometimes approach his wife out of jealousy to gossip that he is dating other actresses. He says he does date some of them, adding: "My wife knows about it and doesn't fight with them."

He also claimed that after late-night productions, some actresses sleep at his house, and that his wife "feeds and takes care of them despite knowing that I'm dating them."

These are Quadri's own claims from a single interview. The report includes no independent verification and no response from the people involved.
