Wesley Varsity Graduates 742 Students As 79 Earn First-Class Degrees

By Trending9ja Staff • November 20, 2026 • 1 min read

Wesley University in Ondo State has announced the graduation of 742 students as part of its 15th convocation ceremonies. According to Punch, 79 members of the graduating class completed their studies with first-class honours.

Speaking with journalists on Friday ahead of Saturday's main event, the Vice-Chancellor, Professor Samuel Obeka, detailed the breakdown of the graduating class. He explained that 434 students completed undergraduate programmes, while 308 earned postgraduate degrees.

Regarding the undergraduate performance, Professor Obeka stated that 283 students earned second-class upper division degrees, 71 received second-class lower degrees, and one student finished with a third-class degree.

The Vice-Chancellor noted that expanding academic offerings in highly sought-after professional fields remains a top focus for his leadership. He pointed to newly accredited courses in the Faculty of Engineering as well as the College of Medical and Allied Health Sciences as major achievements.

He added that the university is strengthening existing programmes across several departments, including education, arts, agriculture, and sciences. This effort aims to keep the institution's curriculum aligned with societal needs, technological growth, and national development goals.

The convocation events also feature the commissioning of several new infrastructure projects to support research and administration. These facilities include a radio studio, a pharmacy building, an anatomy extension, a CBT and ICT complex, an engineering block, a vice-chancellor's residence, and a medical sciences complex.

Sources: Punch
