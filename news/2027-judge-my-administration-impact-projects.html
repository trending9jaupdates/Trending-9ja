2027: Judge My Administration By Impact And Projects, Tinubu Tells Nigerians

By Trending9ja Staff • November 12, 2026 • 1 min read

President Bola Tinubu has called on citizens to evaluate his government based on completed infrastructure projects and the direct impact of its policies on people's lives. According to Daily Post, the message was delivered by Vice-President Kashim Shettima during an All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign gathering in Benin City, Edo State, on Thursday.

Speaking at the event, Vice-President Shettima detailed several achievements recorded over the past three years. He appealed to residents of Edo State to back Tinubu in the upcoming presidential poll set for January 16, 2027, explaining that the administration requires a fresh mandate to conclude ongoing developments and fulfill its pledges.

Shettima also referenced efforts regarding the recovery of stolen historical artifacts belonging to the Benin Kingdom. He noted that institutions in Sweden and Cambridge have transferred ownership of Benin treasures to Nigeria, praising Oba Ewuare II and others who consistently championed the cause over generations.

In his remarks, Edo State Governor Monday Okpebholo pledged to secure 2.5 million votes for President Tinubu in the 2027 election. Okpebholo stated that his state government pays salaries and pensions without delay while carrying out extensive road and infrastructure projects across Edo.

Furthermore, Senate President Godswill Akpabio guaranteed solid backing for Tinubu from the South-South geopolitical zone, describing Edo as a key fortress for the ruling party. Imo State Governor Hope Uzodimma also urged citizens to obtain their Permanent Voter Cards to ensure their political support counts during the polls.

Sources: Daily Post
