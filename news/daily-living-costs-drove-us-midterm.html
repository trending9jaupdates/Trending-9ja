Daily Living Costs Drove US Midterm Voting, Says Nigerian-American Lawmaker Adeyina

By Trending9ja Staff • November 6, 2026 • 1 min read

Segun Adeyina, a Nigerian-American lawmaker re-elected to the Georgia House of Representatives, has stated that American voters focused on personal economic survival rather than political slogans during the US midterm elections.

Speaking on Channels Television’s Sunrise Daily program on Friday, Adeyina explained that citizens cast their ballots based on practical daily concerns. He noted that key issues on voters' minds included their ability to pay rent, afford mortgages, buy groceries, and secure healthcare.

According to Channels TV, the politician asserted that affordability challenges heavily influenced the outcome of the elections. He suggested that President Donald Trump's administration failed to address these rising expenses, adding that involvement in the conflict in Iran further heightened living costs for average American households.

Adeyina described the Democratic victory as a massive outcome, referring to it as a "blue tsunami" driven by public discontent over financial strains. Reflecting on his own victory in Georgia's House District 110—which covers areas such as Grayson, Lawrenceville, Loganville, and Snellville—he stated that his re-election to a third term demonstrated the trust his constituents have in his service since his initial 2022 election.

The Nigerian-American politician was not the only person of Nigerian heritage to achieve electoral success. Channels TV reported that several other Nigerian-Americans won legislative and local government seats across various states, including Will Jawando, Junior Ezeonu, Oye Owolewa, Solomon Adesanya, Carol Kazeem, and Gab Okoye in Maryland, Texas, Washington, D.C., Georgia, and Pennsylvania.

Sources: Channels TV
