Fulham Block Calvin Bassey's January Transfer to Manchester United

By Trending9ja Staff • November 28, 2026 • 1 min read

Fulham head coach Alvaro Arbeloa has shut down rumours linking Nigerian defender Calvin Bassey with a mid-season move to Manchester United, according to Legit.ng. The stance effectively puts an end to speculation regarding the Super Eagles star's potential departure during the January transfer window.

According to reports cited by the news outlet, Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino had identified the 26-year-old as a priority target to reinforce his backline. The Red Devils were specifically searching for a flexible left-sided centre-back who can also operate at left-back, with Bassey valued at around £50 million.

Addressing media representatives ahead of Fulham's league match against Bournemouth, Arbeloa declared that the defender is not up for sale. He stressed that Bassey feels happy at Craven Cottage and remains a vital leader and squad captain whose contributions extend into the dressing room.

Since making his switch to the London outfit from Dutch giants Ajax in 2023, the Nigerian international has established himself as an essential component of Fulham's defensive structure. His impressive physical presence, ability in individual duels, and composure in possession have solidified his role in the team.

To further secure his future, Fulham are actively working to finalize a long-term contract extension with the defender. This ongoing contract negotiation is expected to complicate any prospective transfer bids from Manchester United or other interested clubs if they attempt to revisit the deal during the summer.

Sources: Legit.ng
