FG Unveils Three New CNG Infrastructure Facilities in Kano

By Trending9ja Staff • November 11, 2026 • 1 min read

The Federal Government has inaugurated three Compressed Natural Gas facilities across Kano State in a bid to provide cheaper, cleaner fuel alternatives and cut travel expenses for residents, Channels TV reports.

Rolling Energy Limited constructed the sites with backing from the Midstream and Downstream Gas Infrastructure Fund. The newly opened infrastructure includes a 300,000-standard cubic metres daily Liquefied Compressed Natural Gas distribution hub and retail point at Tamburawa, located in Dawakin Kudu Local Government Area.

A second location consists of a 21,000-standard cubic metres per day daughter and retail outlet situated in the Hotoro district of Tarauni Local Government Area. Authorities stated that the Tamburawa setup incorporates regasification equipment and liquefied natural gas storage to feed retail sites and supply daughter stations directly.

During the launch, the Minister of State for Petroleum Resources (Gas), Ekperikpe Ekpo, speaking through a government representative, explained that the venture reflects an ongoing effort to convert domestic gas reserves into tangible financial benefits for everyday Nigerians.

Ekpo noted that the project is in line with the administration's plan under President Bola Tinubu to shift from policy proposals to physical infrastructure delivery. He added that the administration remains keen on attracting private sector capital to speed up the expansion of gas networks.

Also addressing the gathering through a technical adviser, the head of the Presidential Initiative on Compressed Natural Gas and Electric Vehicles, Ismaeel Ahmed, remarked that the launch follows another recent facility opening by Rolling Energy in Karu, Abuja. He described Kano as a crucial location for expanding gas access because of its vast population and bustling commercial transport trade.

Sources: Channels TV
