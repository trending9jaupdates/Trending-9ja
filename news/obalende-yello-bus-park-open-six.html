Obalende Y'ello Bus Park To Open In Six Weeks, Says Lagos Government

By Trending9ja Staff • November 20, 2026 • 1 min read

The Lagos State Government has announced that the upgraded Obalende Bus Park, also called the Y’ello Bus Park, is scheduled for completion within a six-week window.

The Environment and Water Resources Commissioner, Tokunbo Wahab, made this known during an inspection of the project site alongside other government officials. According to Vanguard, the infrastructure project is being built by telecom firm MTN Nigeria as part of its corporate social responsibility drive.

Wahab explained that the construction suffered delays due to logistics issues involving existing commercial operators on the grounds. To address the challenge, state authorities met with transport unions and contractors to temporarily shift operators away from the active work zone.

When fully finished, the modern hub will hold about 1,000 buses, dedicated loading bays, driver resting quarters, public restrooms, and green spaces. The design also features removable perimeter barriers to allow government teams regular access for cleaning the adjacent drainage canal.

Addressing concerns over potential charges, the Special Adviser to the Governor on Transportation, Sola Giwa, confirmed that using the terminal will be completely toll-free. He added that the facility remains state property under ministry supervision, warning bus drivers to desist from roadside loading once operations begin.

Sources: Vanguard
