AFCON 2027 Qualifier: Éric Chelle Praises Tolu Arokodare After Brace Against Tanzania

By Trending9ja Staff • November 14, 2026 • 1 min read

Super Eagles manager Éric Chelle praised striker Tolu Arokodare after his two goals guided Nigeria to a 3-1 victory against Tanzania in a 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying match, Daily Post reported.

The fixture took place on Friday night at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo, strengthening the national team's prospects of reaching the continental tournament.

Daily Post detailed that the 25-year-old attacker began the match on the substitute bench before coming on to change the course of the game. He put Nigeria ahead 2-1 in the 51st minute by converting a pass provided by team captain Wilfred Ndidi.

Arokodare extended the lead in the 60th minute after controlling a cross from Moses Usor and finishing calmly. The double brought his international record to four goals across 13 appearances for the Super Eagles.

Speaking to reporters after the contest, Chelle stated that bringing on the striker gave the squad a fresh attacking dimension, adding that entering as a substitute and scoring twice required immense quality, confidence, and resolve.

Sources: Daily Post
