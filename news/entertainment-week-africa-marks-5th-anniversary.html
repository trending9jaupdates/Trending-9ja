Entertainment Week Africa Marks 5th Anniversary With Multi-Disciplinary Event in Lagos

By Trending9ja Staff • November 25, 2026 • 1 min read

The fifth anniversary edition of Entertainment Week Africa (EWA) has concluded in Lagos, attracting more than 19,000 physical and virtual attendees. According to BellaNaija, the six-day event brought together creators and applicants from 121 countries and territories, marking the first time the organizers streamed sessions live to a global audience.

The festivities kicked off at the Livespot Entertarium, where 16 emerging musical acts performed before industry stakeholders. The opening evening featured a 30-minute preview of 'University of Side Hustle,' the inaugural scripted feature film from Livespot Studios, authored by Dami Elebe and executive produced by Deola Art Alade.

Daytime activities held at EbonyLife Place covered seven distinct tracks, including Film & TV, Music, Fashion, AI & Tech, and Creative Enterprise. Notable sessions included an animation masterclass anchored by Nissi Ogulu, discussions on elevating local stories internationally, and practical workshops alongside live podcast tapings.

During the event's runway competition, fashion brand Hooked By Lade captured first place along with a ₦2 million prize, while Estaz and Melira took second and third positions, winning ₦1 million and ₦500,000 respectively. Additionally, an art exhibition curated by Jumoke Sanwo alongside the Revolving Art Incubator highlighted works from 21 Nigerian artists and seven designers.

The programme also featured music production workshops with Grammy-nominated duo Banx & Ranx in the sound lab, as well as film showcases presented by the British Council. Reflecting on the milestone, EWA Founder Deola Art Alade noted that the platform was established to provide African talent with the necessary structure to expand their careers globally.

Sources: BellaNaija
