Zlatan Slams Fans Who Behave Badly Around Celebrities: "Fame Should Not Cost You Your Sense of Self"

By Trending9ja Staff • October 7, 2026 • 2 min read

Rapper Zlatan Ibile has criticised people who act excessively around famous figures. In a social media post on Tuesday evening, he said fame should not cost anyone their sense of self, and urged fans to avoid making themselves a subject of ridicule online.

He added that he does not entirely blame those whose behaviour is shaped by how they view celebrity status.
