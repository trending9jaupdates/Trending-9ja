Opposition Parties Set Abuja Summit Date To Sign Unity Agreement

By Trending9ja Staff • November 17, 2026 • 1 min read

Nigeria's opposition coalition has scheduled a gathering for 23 November at the NAF Conference Centre located in Abuja to formalise mutual cooperation, according to Channels TV. The planned summit aims to move the political parties past informal dialogues toward documented commitments ahead of future polls.

The umbrella body, named Coalition for Democracy and previously referred to as G100, stated that attendees will adopt a National Reform Programme. They are also expected to endorse the National Covenant for Democratic Renewal as well as a Framework for Opposition Unity, with safeguarding votes cast at polling stations marked as an initial joint priority.

Parties listed as participants include the ADC, APM, NDC, PDP, PRP, and SDP, although earlier accounts mentioned a wider roster of nine groups. Channels TV reported that while a date has been fixed, the various political platforms have not settled their conflicting interests regarding presidential tickets.

Internal preferences differ widely among the groups. NDC national publicity secretary Osa Director indicated his party would rather have other opposition figures unite behind a ticket featuring Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso, while declining to confirm any separate bilateral talks.

Discussions surrounding other leading figures also remain open. Representatives from the ADC and allies of Atiku Abubakar confirmed ongoing cross-party talks without concrete agreements, while Oyo APM spokesman Michael Ogunsina noted that talks involving Seyi Makinde remain unresolved as Makinde insists on pursuing the presidency for the South. Meanwhile, the PRP under Hakeem Baba-Ahmed has rejected collapsing its party structures into any rival platform.

Sources: Channels TV
