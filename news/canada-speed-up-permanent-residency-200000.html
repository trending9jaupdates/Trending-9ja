Canada to Speed Up Permanent Residency for 200,000 Foreigners in 2027

By Trending9ja Staff • November 11, 2026 • 1 min read

The Canadian government has revealed plans to expedite permanent residency processing for up to 200,000 foreign nationals currently residing within the country in 2027, according to Vanguard.

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada announced the strategy under its 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan. The measure aims to clear growing application backlogs, offer greater stability to newcomers, and address ongoing workforce shortages across various regions.

Under the arrangement, the available slots will be divided equally into two streams: a worker segment and a humanitarian segment, with each allocated up to 100,000 spaces. Vanguard reports that eligible workers will include individuals applying through regional schemes such as the Provincial Nominee Program, the Atlantic Immigration Program, Home Care Worker pathways, and the Start-Up Visa scheme.

The humanitarian allocation will target residents awaiting long-standing immigration decisions, including those pursuing family reunification. Canadian officials noted that the initiative seeks to lower waiting periods for spouses and partners of citizens to roughly 12 months for destinations outside Quebec.

Authorities clarified that individuals already qualifying under these specified categories do not need to submit fresh applications. However, Vanguard highlighted that this offer strictly applies to people already living in Canada and will not serve as a new pathway for candidates applying from abroad.

These 200,000 positions will remain separate from Canada's regular permanent residence target of 380,000 set for 2027. Under the broader multi-year roadmap, the country also intends to welcome 370,000 temporary residents that year, comprising 220,000 foreign workers and 150,000 international students.

Sources: Vanguard
