I Am A Godfather But Don't Meddle In Abiodun's Govt — Osoba

By Trending9ja Staff • November 10, 2026 • 1 min read

Former Ogun State Governor, Chief Olusegun Osoba, has described himself as a political godfather but insisted that he does not meddle in the administration of Governor Dapo Abiodun. Osoba disclosed that despite giving guidance, he has never pushed any individual for a political appointment under the present state executive.

Speaking during an appearance on The Reuben and Kike Abati Podcast, the veteran politician clarified his stance on godfatherism and political influence. According to Punch, Osoba stated that his decision to refrain from pushing nominees into Governor Abiodun's government has helped maintain stability.

The former governor explained that recommending specific individuals for cabinet or government positions would cause disaffection among his numerous supporters. He noted that selecting one person from a large group of loyalists would alienate others and make the appointee a target of suspicion within the administration.

Osoba advised that elected leaders should ensure political associates who assisted during campaigns are rewarded, while retaining the final authority on appointments. He stressed that while senior political figures can offer guidance, they must refrain from dictating how elected officials run their governments.

Furthermore, the elder statesman publicly declared Senator Solomon Olamilekan Adeola as his preferred political candidate. Osoba highlighted Adeola’s extensive legislative background in the Lagos State House of Assembly, the House of Representatives, and the Senate, alongside his financial background as an accountant.

Osoba served as governor of Ogun State twice, first from 1992 to 1993, and again between 1999 and 2003. Governor Dapo Abiodun assumed office in 2019 after succeeding former governor Ibikunle Amosun.

Sources: Punch
