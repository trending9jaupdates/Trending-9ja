59 Graduate With First Class as AE-FUNAI Prepares for Combined Convocation

By Trending9ja Staff • November 24, 2026 • 1 min read

Alex Ekwueme Federal University, Ndufu-Alike Ikwo in Ebonyi State has recorded 59 first-class degree graduates across four academic sessions, according to Vanguard.

The institution's Vice Chancellor, Professor Daniel Nwachukwu, revealed the figures during a pre-convocation media briefing. He stated that a total of 8,649 undergraduate students are set to graduate, comprising 1,867 second-class upper division, 5,040 second-class lower division, 1,675 third-class, and eight pass degree holders.

Vanguard reported that an additional 552 postgraduate students will graduate, including 44 PhD, 348 M.Sc., and 160 Postgraduate Diploma candidates. Professor Nwachukwu, who took office ten months ago as the fourth substantive head of the university, noted that this marks the first convocation under his administration.

The Vice Chancellor outlined activities scheduled for the combined convocation event, which include a research and innovation fair, an alumni dinner and awards ceremony, and a Chancellor’s ball in honour of the Alake of Egbaland, Oba Michael Aremu Adedotun Gbadebo. A convocation lecture will also be delivered by the pioneer chairman of Seplat Energies Ltd, Dr. ABC Orjiakor.

Furthermore, the university plans to bestow honorary doctorate degrees on three prominent Nigerians for their contributions to national growth: MTN Nigeria Chairman Dr. Ernest Ndukwe, Dr. ABC Orjiakor, and Renaissance Energy founder Dr. Olayiwola Francis Fatona.

Sources: Vanguard
