Segun Sowunmi Explains Delay in Officially Registering With APC

By Trending9ja Staff • November 16, 2026 • 1 min read

Segun Sowunmi, a former chieftain of the Peoples Democratic Party, has revealed the reason he has not yet officially registered as a member of the ruling All Progressives Congress, despite his declared support for President Bola Tinubu.

Speaking on Channels Television’s Politics Today broadcast on Monday, Sowunmi explained that he wants to preserve a level of political autonomy. He noted that remaining outside the ruling party allows him the freedom to publicly critique the administration when things go wrong.

According to Daily Post, the former PDP leader stated that many citizens expect him to remain a strong opposition voice. He explained that keeping his independence ensures he can offer fair and balanced commentary on national matters while still backing the president.

Sowunmi cited an open letter he wrote to National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu as proof of his non-partisan stance. The letter urged authorities to curb reckless political remarks capable of harming national stability.

Additionally, the politician pointed out that guidelines in the recent Electoral Act have removed any urgent rush to join a new party ahead of upcoming elections. He described the APC as a solid and well-structured political platform, adding that he could still formally enlist when the time is appropriate.

Addressing critics who question his political loyalty, Sowunmi dismissed claims of inconsistency. He insisted that he is not supporting any alternative candidate for the presidency.

Sources: Daily Post
