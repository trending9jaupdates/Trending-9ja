AI in Health Summit Set for 19–20 October in Abuja

By Trending9ja Staff • October 17, 2026 • 3 min read

The Artificial Intelligence in Health Summit is scheduled for 19–20 October in Abuja, bringing together policymakers, innovators and health experts.

Organisers and speakers say AI can help reduce out-of-pocket health spending, improve early detection (including TB and cervical cancer tools), and bridge Nigeria’s health workforce gaps. Mary-Brenda Akoda’s GenMRI technology remains a key reference point for locally developed solutions.

Sources: NAN · Leadership · AHFID
