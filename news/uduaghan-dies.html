Former Delta Governor Emmanuel Uduaghan Dies at 71

By Trending9ja Staff • October 19, 2026 • 2 min read

Former Delta State Governor Dr Emmanuel Eweta Uduaghan has died in Abuja at the age of 71, just days before his birthday. President Tinubu and the Nigeria Governors’ Forum have paid tribute, describing him as a dedicated public servant.
