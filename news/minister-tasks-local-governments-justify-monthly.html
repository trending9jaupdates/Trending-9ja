Minister Tasks Local Governments to Justify Monthly Allocations With Projects

By Trending9ja Staff • November 12, 2026 • 1 min read

The Minister of Budget and Economic Planning, Senator Abubakar Atiku Bagudu, has called on local government administrations across Nigeria to justify their monthly Federation Account allocations by delivering visible development projects instead of merely distributing funds, according to Vanguard.

Bagudu made the remarks on Thursday in Abuja during the ninth national conference organised by the Association of Local Government Vice Chairmen of Nigeria (ALGOVC). The minister noted that councils must discover their local economic advantages, draw private investments, and build communal wealth rather than surviving only on statutory monthly disbursements.

He explained that the Federal Government is implementing a bottom-up model via the Renewed Hope Ward Development Plan to identify investment possibilities and local assets. According to him, council officials should view their offices as hubs for unlocking community potential rather than simple disbursement centres, urging local councillors to properly map resources across wards to attract investors.

The minister specifically pointed to agriculture, livestock, and fisheries as key sectors that could transform grassroots economies, noting that poor techniques and inadequate inputs currently hinder productivity for roughly 42 million farming households. He advised the council leaders to present evidence-based requests to federal authorities and international partners.

Earlier at the gathering, the National Chairperson of ALGOVC, Ambassador Uju Okolo, called on members to embrace inclusive leadership, accountability, and unity. The event later adopted a motion moved by the group's Board of Trustees Chairman, Lawrence Onuchukwu, and supported by Okolo, formally endorsing President Bola Tinubu for a second term in 2027.

Sources: Vanguard
