Olufunso Amosun Empowers Osun Youths With AI Skills, N5m Grants

By Trending9ja Staff • November 9, 2026 • 1 min read

Mrs Olufunso Amosun, the former First Lady of Ogun State, has provided digital skills and Artificial Intelligence training to 70 young people in Ile-Ife, Osun State, according to Punch. She also distributed N5 million in financial support to traders, widows, and other vulnerable citizens in the area.

Punch reported that the empowerment scheme was organized to mark the 2026 Edi Moremi Festival. Alongside the training and cash grants, the initiative provided free health screening and eye checks for senior citizens living in the community.

The 70 beneficiaries formed the pioneer group of participants who completed a six-week program at a dedicated hub commissioned in late September. They received their completion certificates at a ceremony held over the weekend.

Speaking at the event, Amosun stated that the project was inspired by the bravery and selflessness of Queen Moremi Ajasoro. She emphasized that honoring the legendary queen requires taking concrete steps to uplift members of the public and boost community growth.

The former First Lady explained that financial constraints often prevent talented youths, school leavers, and unemployed graduates from acquiring modern tech skills. She noted that the newly established center aims to bridge this gap by offering free training to about 200 individuals every six weeks.

Amosun urged the graduates to apply the practical knowledge they acquired rather than merely relying on their certificates, stressing that personal dedication is essential for achieving long-term success.

Sources: Punch
