Australia Details Age Brackets Exempt from Citizenship Test

By Trending9ja Staff • November 11, 2026 • 1 min read

The Australian authorities have outlined clear age exemptions that allow certain immigrants to bypass the mandatory citizenship examination, according to Legit.ng.

Official guidance shows that any applicant aged 17 or below when filing their paperwork is fully excused from writing the assessment. This rule applies to everyone in that age group without additional prerequisites.

Similarly, people who have reached at least 60 years of age at the time of their submission do not need to undergo the test. The government indicated that the rules are designed to assist young candidates as well as seniors who might face undue hardship.

For individuals aged between 18 and 59, taking the citizenship test remains an obligatory step in the process. Authorities encouraged eligible applicants to verify their qualification status so their documentation accurately reflects the exemption.

The report added that Australia recognizes seven total exemption categories for the evaluation. Beyond age criteria, waivers also apply to individuals with particular physical or mental impairments, as well as persons meeting specific birth or nationality conditions.

Sources: Legit.ng
