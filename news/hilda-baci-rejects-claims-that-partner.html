Hilda Baci Rejects Claims That Partner Dapper Made Her Successful

By Trending9ja Staff • November 8, 2026 • 1 min read

Renowned Nigerian chef Hilda Baci has voiced her discontent over public claims suggesting that her career accomplishments were largely driven by her romantic partner, according to a report by Punch. The culinary entrepreneur stated that such comments reflect broader societal biases against women who achieve success.

Punch reported that Baci addressed the issue during an interview clip released by Goldmyne TV on Saturday. The record-breaking chef explained that while she would not describe the public commentary as completely infuriating, she finds the claims distressing because of what they imply about female independence.

Reflecting on the situation, Baci noted that the narrative shows the current mindset of many people regarding female accomplishments. She added that the ongoing discourse highlights how much further effort is required to reshape popular views on women building their own careers.

The chef explained that the experience has motivated her to be far more deliberate about how she presents herself to the public. According to the report, Baci expressed a strong desire to represent women effectively while creating opportunities for other females to excel in their pursuits.

Baci's partner, Damilola Akinwunmi, widely known as Dapper, is a music executive and entrepreneur who established Dapper Music alongside Dapper Group.

Punch noted that Baci built her individual national and international reputation through her culinary enterprise and her record-breaking cooking marathon, which earned her a Guinness World Record.

Sources: Punch
