Tinubu Asks Reps to Approve N10.79tn Foreign Borrowing Plan

By Trending9ja Staff • November 11, 2026 • 1 min read

President Bola Tinubu has requested the House of Representatives to approve the execution of a N10.79 trillion ($7.71 billion) foreign loan structure designed to assist in funding the 2026 federal budget deficit.

The request was submitted in a formal correspondence to Speaker Tajudeen Abbas and read out to members during plenary, according to reports by Punch and Vanguard. The requested sum equals $7.707 billion when calculated using the budget benchmark rate of N1,400 to one United States dollar.

Vanguard reported that President Tinubu clarified the letter does not present a new debt request, but rather fulfills a legal requirement to execute borrowing previously included in the 2026 Appropriation Act. He explained that Sections 21(1) and 27(1) of the Debt Management Office Establishment Act 2003 mandate that the specific conditions of foreign loans must receive National Assembly approval.

According to Punch, the overall borrowing target approved for the 2026 fiscal cycle is N29.22 trillion, consisting of N18.28 trillion in domestic debt and N10.79 trillion from international sources. Tinubu stated that the government will evaluate several funding channels, but will place special emphasis on Eurobond issuance due to its speed and relatively lower financing costs.

The President informed the lawmakers that the external loans will be utilized to fund vital projects in key sectors including national defence, transport, agriculture, power, education, and health. He added that receiving the loan proceeds through the Central Bank of Nigeria will help strengthen the country's foreign reserves and support the naira.

Sources: Punch · Vanguard
