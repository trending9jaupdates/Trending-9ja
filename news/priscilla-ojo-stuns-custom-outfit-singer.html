Priscilla Ojo Stuns in Custom Outfit at Singer L.A.X's Wedding Reception

By Trending9ja Staff • November 2, 2026 • 1 min read

Nollywood actress Priscilla Ojo generated significant online attention after attending the wedding reception of Nigerian musician L.A.X and his bride, Emmanuella Damilola, as reported by Legit.ng.

According to the report, Ojo arrived at the event in a custom rose-pink and mint-green traditional gown. The outfit featured an architectural corset bodice with upward-flaring shoulder extensions, crystal brooches along the front, a scalloped peplum, and a mermaid skirt with a sweeping train decorated with rose-gold sequins and mint floral accents. She complemented the attire with a styled mint-green gele.

Images of her attire quickly circulated online, drawing praise from fans and figures in the entertainment industry. Legit.ng noted that celebrity chef Hilda Baci, fashion designer Veekee James, and actress Mercy Aigbe all expressed admiration for Ojo's look through comments on social media.

The reception followed the Islamic Nikkah ceremony of L.A.X, born Damilola Afolabi, and Emmanuella, which brought together several prominent figures from the Nigerian entertainment industry.

Sources: Legit.ng
